About Stormen

Get to know the man behind the moustache.

My Story

Hello! I’m Stormen — full‑time streamer, content creator and proud moustache owner. For almost a decade I’ve been playing competitive FPS games on Twitch and YouTube, balancing high‑skill gameplay with a chilled, welcoming vibe. I believe in making gaming fun and approachable for everyone, regardless of skill level. Whether it’s Apex Legends, Rainbow Six or the latest shooter, I love sharing tips, laughing with chat and creating memorable moments.

Milestones

  • 2016: Started streaming full‑time on Twitch.
  • 2018: Reached 100k followers and hosted my first charity stream.
  • 2020: Launched the Moustache Club merch line and built a thriving community on Discord.
  • 2023: Hit 500k subscribers on YouTube and collabed with top creators.
  • 2025: Released the “Best of 2025” montage that went viral.

Values

My channel is built on three core values: Skill, Community and Fun. I strive to push my gameplay to the next level while maintaining a positive and inclusive environment where everyone can hang out and share laughs. Gaming should be fun — let’s enjoy it together!
